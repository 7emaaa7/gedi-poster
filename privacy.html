Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what data Gedi Poster ("the app") handles. The app is operated by an individual in Germany. Contact: emadavison77@icloud.com.

1. Who uses the app

The app is used by its operator to publish videos on the operator's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

2. Data we process

  • TikTok authorization data: after the operator connects the account through TikTok Login, the app receives an access token and a refresh token, and the basic profile information that the granted scopes allow.
  • Video content: the video files, captions and hashtags that the operator uploads for publishing.

3. How we use the data

The data is used only to upload and publish the operator's videos through the TikTok Content Posting API and to notify the operator when a post has been published. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok, as needed to publish the videos.

5. Storage and retention

Tokens and video files are stored in access-protected systems controlled by the operator. Tokens are deleted when the operator disconnects the app. Video files are deleted after publishing.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing. Contact us at the address above. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
